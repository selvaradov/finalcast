# Popularity vs Difficulty/Volatility

n = 79 papers with both fitted distributions and candidate count data.

| Relationship | Pearson r | p-value | Spearman ρ | p-value |
|-------------|-----------|---------|-----------|---------|
| Popularity vs Mean (μ) | -0.279 | 0.0127 | -0.287 | 0.0104 |
| Popularity vs Volatility (σ) | 0.317 | 0.0044 | 0.319 | 0.0042 |

## Interpretation

- More popular papers tend to be **harder** (r=-0.279, p=0.0127).
- More popular papers tend to be **more volatile** (r=0.317, p=0.0044).
