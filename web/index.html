How it works

Each paper's mark distribution is modelled as:

mark_i = μ_i + θ + ε_i

  • μ_i — paper-specific mean, fitted via MLE on truncated normals from 15 years of examiners' report band data.
  • θ ~ N(0, σ²_ability) — shared latent ability factor (σ ≈ 2.7), capturing correlation across papers.
  • ε_i — paper-specific noise, variance = σ²_paper − σ²_ability.

The ability slider shifts all paper means by Φ⁻¹(percentile) × σ_ability. The simulation draws 50,000 samples, classifies each using official PPE exam conventions (conjunctive rules on averages and paper counts), and reports the empirical distribution.

Limitations: uses aggregate band data (not individual marks), assumes conditional independence of ε terms. Estimates are priors, not predictions.
