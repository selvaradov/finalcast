How it works

Each paper's mark distribution is modelled using a latent ability factor model. The idea: students have some underlying ability level, and each paper's marks reflect that ability plus paper-specific noise.

  • Paper fits: 79 papers fitted to truncated normal distributions using data in examiners' reports from 2011–2025, covering candidates all the way back to 2005.
  • Simulation: we sample 50,000 sittings, and classify each using the examination conventions to produce probability distributions over outcomes.
  • Uncertainty: estimates are accurate to ~±3 percentage points, and do not account for selection bias or other confounders.
