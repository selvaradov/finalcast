# The Big Picture

15 years of Oxford PPE results at a glance.

## First-class rate over time

Percentage of candidates awarded a First, 2005–2025. The 2020 spike is COVID, not a cohort change.

## Gender gap

First-class rate by gender where data is available. The gap has been persistent at roughly 8–10 percentage points.

## Subject comparison

Weighted averages across all papers in each subject.

## Classification breakdown

Full distribution of results each year.

## Score trends

Fitted mean marks for papers with statistically significant drift (p<0.05). Most papers are stable — only 3 of 65 show significant trends. Gaps: 2020 excluded (COVID), 2023 has no per-paper data (marking boycott). Phil Logic and Thesis weren't reported in 2024–25.

## Popularity trends

Average annual growth rate in candidate numbers for papers with a significant trend (p<0.1). Excludes 2020 and 2023. Sorted by growth rate.

## COVID 2020

40% Firsts. Marking scale was unchanged, but classification conventions were modified as a safety net: the lowest two passing results were dropped when calculating the classification average. Per-paper means shifted by only +0.3 marks — the doubled First rate came entirely from the classification rule change. 2020 is excluded from all model fitting.

## Kingmaker papers

Papers with σ ≥ 10 — high enough variance that a single sitting can swing you up or down a full class.
